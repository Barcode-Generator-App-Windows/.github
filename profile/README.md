## 🚀 What It Is
Barcode Generator for Windows is a desktop app that turns your numbers, text, and links into scannable codes. It removes the hassle of stitching together spreadsheet tricks or online tools when you need reliable barcodes for products, tickets, or inventory. As a qr barcode generator it also handles 2D formats, so a single install covers both linear labels and modern square codes on your PC.

## ✨ Key Features
> Everything you need to design, generate, and output barcodes lives in one window.

- 🔳 **2D and QR support** — Create QR and other 2D barcode formats for links, contacts, and more.
- 📏 **1D symbologies** — Generate the common linear barcode types used for retail and inventory.
- 🔁 **Batch runs** — Produce many codes at once with random or sequential data for whole label sets.
- 🖨️ **Print or save** — Send barcodes straight to a printer or export them as image files.
- 🎚️ **Fine control** — Adjust dimensions, quiet zones, and captions to fit your label stock.
